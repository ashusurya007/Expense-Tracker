Quick Add

Budgets

Monthly limits per category. They reset on the 1st and unused money doesn't carry over. Credit card bill payments, cash withdrawals and bank transfers aren't counted.

Recurring

Rent, EMIs, salary, subscriptions. The app can only remind you when it's open, and it never adds anything by itself — you confirm each one.

Money lent / owed

Money you lent or borrowed moves your cash and bank balances, but it is not an expense or income — unless you write it off or it's forgiven.

Balance Adjustments

Adjustments correct small drift between what this app calculates and what your bank or wallet actually shows (interest, bank charges, rounding, etc). They affect your Bank/Cash balance but are kept out of your spending categories and Lifetime Stats.
